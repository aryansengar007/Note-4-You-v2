Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This page is a personal note written and run by Aryan Sengar, an individual. It is not a business, and it does not collect information about you.

What this page does not do

  • It has no accounts, forms, or sign-ups.
  • It sets no cookies and does not use local storage.
  • It has no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • It does not load anything from other companies. The fonts and styles are stored on the same site.

Your choices stay on your phone

When you tap Yes or No, the page changes in your browser. Nothing about that choice is sent to me or stored anywhere. If you reload the page, it starts again from the beginning.

What the hosting provider can see

Like any website, the company that hosts this page may keep standard server records, such as the time of a visit, your IP address, and your browser type. I do not use these records to identify who opened the page, and I do not look at them to find you.

If you choose to message me

The note only offers contact options if you ask for them. When you say yes to the photo, the page puts those words into a new WhatsApp or email draft, which you can edit before sending. For Instagram and Snapchat, which cannot pre-fill a message, the page copies those words to your clipboard so you can paste them. Nothing is sent until you press send yourself, and the copied words never leave your device through this page.

Once you leave this page, the other service has its own privacy rules. I will see your name or account and your message only if you send one. I will not share it, and I will delete it if you ask.

Questions

You can ask a question about this page through the contact options on the note, if you choose to see them.
